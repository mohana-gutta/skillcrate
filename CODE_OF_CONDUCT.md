# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive, especially in code review.
- Give and accept feedback graciously; assume good intent.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, derogatory comments, or personal or political attacks
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions) and when someone represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainer, [@mohana-gutta](https://github.com/mohana-gutta), by contacting them through their GitHub profile. Do not post reports publicly. Maintainers will review every report promptly and fairly, and will respect the privacy of the reporter.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor for behavior they deem inappropriate, threatening or harmful. Enforcement follows the [Contributor Covenant enforcement guidelines](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/#enforcement-guidelines): correction, warning, temporary ban, then permanent ban.
